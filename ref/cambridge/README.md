## Book Summaries & Detailed Tables of Contents

### 1. [Understanding Machine Learning: From Theory to Algorithms](./understanding-machine-learning.pdf)
* **Authors:** Shai Shalev-Shwartz and Shai Ben-David
* **Brief Intro:** Bridges fundamental learning theory with practical algorithms, covering PAC learning, VC-dimension, convex optimization, and deep architectures.
* **Table of Contents:**
  * Part I: Foundations (A Gentle Start, A Formal Learning Model, Learning via Uniform Convergence, The Bias-Complexity Tradeoff, The VC-Dimension, Non-uniform Learnability, The Runtime of Learning)
  * Part II: From Theory to Algorithms (Linear Predictors, Boosting, Model Selection and Validation, Convex Learning Problems, Regularization and Stability, Stochastic Gradient Descent, Support Vector Machines, Kernel Methods, Multiclass and Complex Prediction, Decision Trees, Nearest Neighbor, Neural Networks)
  * Part III: Additional Learning Models (Online Learning, Clustering, Dimensionality Reduction, Generative Models, Feature Selection and Generation)
  * Part IV: Advanced Theory (Rademacher Complexities, Covering Numbers, Proof of the Fundamental Theorem, Multiclass Learnability, Compression Bounds, PAC-Bayes)

### 2. [Foundations of Machine Learning](./foundations-of-machine-learning.pdf)
* **Authors:** Mehryar Mohri, Afshin Rostamizadeh, and Ameet Talwalkar
* **Brief Intro:** Provides a comprehensive mathematical treatment of modern machine learning algorithms and core theoretical paradigms.
* **Table of Contents:**
  * Introduction and PAC Learning Framework
  * Rademacher Complexity and VC-Dimension
  * Support Vector Machines and Kernel Methods
  * Boosting and Decision Trees
  * Regression Analysis and Generalization Bounds
  * Algorithmic Stability and Regularization
  * Reinforcement Learning and Online Learning

### 3. [Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning](./pattern-recognition-and-machine-learning.pdf)
* **Authors:** Christopher M. Bishop
* **Brief Intro:** The definitive Bayesian perspective text on statistical pattern recognition, inference, and graphical models.
* **Table of Contents:**
  * Introduction (Probability, Model Selection, Curse of Dimensionality)
  * Probability Distributions (Gaussian, Binomial, Multinomial, Nonparametric)
  * Linear Models for Regression and Classification
  * Neural Networks and Kernel Methods
  * Mixture Models and Expectation-Maximization (EM)
  * Graphical Models and Approximate Inference
  * Sampling Methods and Continuous Latent Variables

### 4. [Elements of Statistical Learning](./elements-of-statistical-learning.pdf)
* **Authors:** Trevor Hastie, Robert Tibshirani, and Jerome Friedman
* **Brief Intro:** Focuses on the statistical foundations of data mining, inference, prediction, and unsupervised learning.
* **Table of Contents:**
  * Overview of Supervised Learning (Linear Regression, KNN)
  * Linear Methods for Regression and Classification
  * Kernel Smoothing Methods and Model Assessment
  * Additive Models, Trees, and Boosting
  * Support Vector Machines and Neural Networks
  * Unsupervised Learning (Cluster Analysis, PCA, Self-Organizing Maps)
  * Random Forests and Ensemble Methods

### 5. [Deep Learning](./deep-learning.pdf)
* **Authors:** Ian Goodfellow, Yoshua Bengio, and Aaron Courville
* **Brief Intro:** An authoritative guide on deep feedforward networks, optimization, regularization, and practical applications.
* **Table of Contents:**
  * Applied Math and Machine Learning Basics
  * Deep Networks: Feedforward Networks, Regularization, and Optimization
  * Convolutional Networks (CNNs) and Sequence Modeling (RNNs)
  * Autoencoders and Representation Learning
  * Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) and Deep Generative Models

### 6. [Probabilistic Graphical Models](./probabilistic-graphical-models.pdf)
* **Authors:** Daphne Koller and Nir Friedman
* **Brief Intro:** Principles and techniques for managing uncertainty using graph-structured probability distributions.
* **Table of Contents:**
  * Directed Graphical Models (Bayesian Networks)
  * Undirected Graphical Models (Markov Networks)
  * Exact and Approximate Inference algorithms
  * Learning Parameters and Structure in Graphical Models
  * Decision Theory and Partially Observable Markov Decision Processes (POMDPs)

### 7. [Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction](./reinforcement-learning.pdf)
* **Authors:** Richard S. Sutton and Andrew G. Barto
* **Brief Intro:** The standard text on computational approaches to learning through trial-and-error interactions.
* **Table of Contents:**
  * Tabular Solution Methods (Multi-arm Bandits, MDPs, Dynamic Programming)
  * Monte Carlo Methods and Temporal-Difference Learning
  * n-step Bootstrapping and Planning with Approximations
  * On-policy and Off-policy Function Approximation
  * Policy Gradient Methods and Contemporary Applications

### 8. [Machine Learning: A Probabilistic Perspective](./machine-learning-probabilistic-perspective.pdf)
* **Authors:** Kevin P. Murphy
* **Brief Intro:** Comprehensive introduction to machine learning using probabilistic modeling as a unifying framework.
* **Table of Contents:**
  * Foundations (Probability, Generative/Discriminative Models)
  * Linear Regression, Logistic Regression, and Generalized Linear Models
  * Bayesian Statistics and Gaussian Processes
  * Adaptive Computation, Clustering, and Latent Variable Models
  * Deep Learning, Graphical Models, and Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC)

### 9. [Computer Vision: Algorithms and Applications](./computer-vision-algorithms-and-applications.pdf)
* **Authors:** Richard Szeliski
* **Brief Intro:** Explores image processing, feature extraction, alignment, stereo matching, and modern recognition algorithms.
* **Table of Contents:**
  * Image Processing and Feature Detection
  * Image Alignment and Stitching
  * Structure from Motion and Multi-View Geometry
  * Dense Motion Estimation and Image Segmentation
  * Recognition, Deep Learning for Vision, and 3D Reconstruction

### 10. [Convex Optimization](./convex-optimization.pdf)
* **Authors:** Stephen Boyd and Lieven Vandenberghe
* **Brief Intro:** Comprehensive guide to convex sets, functions, optimization problems, duality, and algorithmic applications in machine learning.
* **Table of Contents:**
  * Convex Sets, Convex Functions, and Canonical Problems
  * Convex Optimization Problems (LP, QP, SOCP, SDP)
  * Duality and Geometric Interpretations
  * Approximation and Fitting (Regularization, Cross-Validation)
  * Interior-Point Methods and Subgradient Algorithms
